Policies & Terms

This page provides information about Elkhoi's service terms, customer responsibilities, payments, cancellations and privacy practices.

Privacy PolicyGeneral TermsCancellationCleaningLaundryMaintenanceProperty ManagementPhoto & Media

1. Privacy Policy

We may collect your name, phone/WhatsApp number, email, location, property details and service requirements when you contact Elkhoi or request a service.

We use this information to respond to enquiries, arrange services, confirm bookings and quotations, communicate with you and coordinate service providers where necessary.

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and do not sell your personal information.

You may contact Elkhoi with questions or requests concerning your personal information.

2. General Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to Cleaning, Laundry, Maintenance and Property Management services.

  • A submitted enquiry is not automatically a confirmed booking.
  • Final prices depend on the actual requirements, condition, location and agreed scope.
  • Additional work, materials, parts or specialist services may be charged separately where applicable.
  • Customers should provide accurate information and reasonable access required for the service.
  • Service concerns should be raised with Elkhoi as soon as reasonably possible.
  • Elkhoi may decline or postpone work where conditions are unsafe or outside the agreed scope.

3. Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Customers should notify Elkhoi as early as possible if they need to cancel or reschedule a confirmed booking or appointment.

  • Cancellation or rescheduling must be made at least one day before the scheduled service date, or on the scheduled service date before 08:30.
  • Requests made after 08:30 on the scheduled service date may be treated as a late cancellation or missed appointment.
  • If Elkhoi needs to reschedule, we will communicate with the customer and arrange another suitable time.
  • Changes to the agreed service may result in a change to the price or scope, with the customer's agreement.

4. Cleaning Policy

  • A cleaning booking is confirmed after Elkhoi confirms the availability, scope and agreed price.
  • 50% is payable before cleaning begins after the team confirms the agreed scope; the remaining 50% is payable on completion.
  • If the property's actual condition differs materially from what was described, any additional work or price adjustment will be discussed before proceeding.
  • Additional cleaning requirements outside the agreed scope may attract additional charges.

5. Laundry Policy

  • Minimum laundry order: 3 kg.
  • Bookings should be made at least 1 day before pickup.
  • At pickup: 50% of the laundry charge plus the full pickup/delivery fee.
  • At delivery: remaining 50% of the laundry charge.
  • Express/Same-day service, where available, attracts +50%.
  • Final weight and price are confirmed by Elkhoi.
  • Customers should identify delicate items and remove valuables from pockets before handing over laundry.

6. Maintenance Policy

Elkhoi manages your maintenance needs from request to completion, using trusted and vetted technical professionals where required.

  • Maintenance work may require an assessment or quotation before work begins.
  • Parts, materials or specialist services may be charged separately.
  • Additional work will be communicated before proceeding where reasonably possible.
  • Customers must provide reasonable access to the property and relevant areas.

7. Property Management Policy

Property Management services are provided according to the specific scope agreed with the customer.

  • Services may include property checks, inspections, upkeep coordination, cleaning coordination, maintenance coordination and reporting.
  • An inspection does not automatically include repairs or cleaning unless included in the agreed service.
  • Customers are responsible for providing necessary property information and access arrangements.
  • Additional services or third-party work will be discussed and agreed separately where applicable.

8. Photo & Media Consent

Where appropriate, Elkhoi may take photographs of completed work for service records.

Use of customer or property photographs for marketing purposes is optional. Customers may decline marketing consent without affecting their ability to request or receive Elkhoi services.

Questions?
For questions about these policies or your service, please contact Elkhoi Home & Property Services using the contact details on our website.
